# Investment criteria

The pipeline's price-discount signals are starting points, not buy or resale recommendations. Every potential opportunity must pass the qualitative review below.

## Disqualifying conditions

1. **Micro-location.** Do not judge location from the neighborhood alone. Risk areas, poor street liquidity, and local context can explain a lower price per square metre.
2. **Parking.** The intended market requires an independent parking space. Tandem, blocked, mixed, unconfirmed, or no-parking listings are not parking-eligible.
3. **Condominium condition.** A low condominium fee is not a substitute for assessing maintenance and buyer perception.
4. **Noise and surroundings.** Persistently noisy streets or problematic surroundings can invalidate an otherwise attractive financial calculation.
5. **Listing consistency.** Area, parking, bedrooms, and other attributes must be consistent with the description and verified before estimating value.

## Parking classification

The processing stage preserves the evidence text and classifies parking as `independent`, `tandem`, `blocked`, `mixed`, `no_space`, or `unknown`. Tandem parking takes precedence over references to an independent space. Listings without explicit evidence remain `pending_review` rather than being inferred as eligible.
